    Methodology & honest disclaimers

    • Testnet — not real money. Order flow is real (Binance Futures Testnet); balances are simulated. This is a transparency record, not a live audited fund.
    • No return promises. Nothing here predicts or guarantees future profit. Past results — including the backtest — do not imply future results.
    • Not financial advice. This is a tool/analytics record, not a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold anything.
    • What the edge actually is: conventional technical analysis + a 4h regime filter (longs only in BULL) + mean-reversion in range + fixed-dollar risk sizing and hard drawdown circuit-breakers. No secret indicators, no exotic alpha claims.
    • Verify it yourself: every number on this page is reconstructable from the bot's raw append-only log.
    • Benchmark methodology: "own-universe HODL" is an equal-weight buy & hold of the bot's own coin universe in USD terms since the current fresh-start epoch (Binance futures 1h closes). It exists to keep comparisons honest — a benchmark in a depreciating fiat currency (e.g. a ₺-denominated chart) can make "the market" look up when in USD it is not.
